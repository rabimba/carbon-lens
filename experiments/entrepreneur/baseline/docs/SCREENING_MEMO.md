## 3. Deep-Dive Kill Reasons on Rejected Candidates

### Candidate 7: State Unclaimed Property / Tax Overages Finder — *Hard Reject (37.8/100)*
- **Why it looks tempting:** Public unclaimed property tables list dollar amounts owed to businesses/individuals.
- **Why it fails catastrophically under our rules:** Many states cap finder fees at 10%, require state Private Investigator licenses to solicit claimants commercially, mandate wet-ink notarized Powers of Attorney, and require live phone calls to convince skeptical owners you aren't running a scam. Founder time easily exceeds 8–15 hours/month.

### Candidate 6: Niche Shopify / Chrome Extension Micro-SaaS — *Hard Reject (48.0/100)*
- **Why it fails:** App store customer acquisition requires ongoing content marketing or paid ads, Shopify GraphQL/webhook deprecations create mandatory maintenance sprints, and \$19/month merchants expect live chat support within hours—shattering the 20-minute monthly cap.

### Candidate 5: EPA ECHO Noncompliance Feed — *Hard Reject (64.5/100)*
- **Why it fails:** Market verification revealed that the US EPA already operates **ECHO Notify**, a free public alerting tool where environmental consultants can subscribe directly to enforcement/violation notifications by ZIP or state. Charging \$250/mo against a free federal alert button creates high churn unless paired with manual engineering analysis.

### Candidate 4: New Restaurant Liquor License + Permit Leads — *Hard Reject (66.8/100)*
- **Why it fails:** Market research confirmed automated state feeds (*New Restaurant Permits*, *RestoSignals*) already sell statewide daily alerts, CSVs, and webhooks for **\$25–\$49/month**. At \$29/mo, hitting \$20,000 net requires ~65+ concurrent subscribers, while charging \$299/mo requires human phone/email skip-tracing that violates our 20-minute founder ceiling.

### Candidate 2: Municipal Decarbonization / BPS Mandate Leads (LL97, BERDO, Energize Denver) — *Runner-Up (78.5/100)*
- **Why it came close:** Steep carbon caps (\$268/tCO2e in NYC LL97; up to \$300/day in Boston BERDO) drive large HVAC retrofit projects.
- **Why CiteSignal beat it:** Capital decarbonization retrofits cost \$250k–\$3M and have 9–18 month committee sales cycles (and cities like Denver deferred performance penalties to late 2029). By contrast, **failed commercial boiler safety inspections, OATH Class-1 elevator/standpipe summonses, and overdue Texas TDLR elevator certificates carry immediate 10-to-90 day statutory deadlines** and \$3,000–\$18,000 service tickets that a Chief Building Engineer can approve out of operating maintenance budgets *this week*.

1. **Demand Side (Urgent Commercial Repairs) Is Public but Dirty:**
